# Literature matrix

These rows are the academic background for the project. They are not Qasper evaluation items. Findings below are taken from the abstract or the opening of the cited source. A limitation is marked **author-reported** only when that source states it. Otherwise the cell says that the checked source did not state one, or it is marked **inferred** from the described setup.

| FActScore: Fine-grained Atomic Evaluation of Factual Precision in Long Form Text Generation | Sewon Min, Kalpesh Krishna, Xinxi Lyu, Mike Lewis, Wen-tau Yih, Pang Koh, Mohit Iyyer, Luke Zettlemoyer, Hannaneh Hajishirzi | 2023 | Score long generations as atomic facts | Break a generation into facts and measure the share supported by a knowledge source | Biographies from commercial LMs; later 6,500 generations | Percentage of supported atomic facts | ChatGPT reaches 58% supported atomic facts in the human study they report.
